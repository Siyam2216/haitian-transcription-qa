How a clip moves through the pipeline

One file, one decision, one label

01 — SPLIT

Dual-channel audio is separated into single-channel clips, one speaker role per clip.

02 — VAD

Clips are cut down to short segments using Voice Activity Detection.

03 — LABEL

You mark each clip Valid or Invalid — one bad segment invalidates the whole file.

04 — TRANSCRIBE / QA

Valid clips are transcribed exactly as spoken; QA checks the output line by line.

The Work

Two passes over the same audio

01

Transcription

Listen to short Kreyòl clips and write exactly what's said — no added or dropped words, fillers standardized (emmm/emm/ehm → one spelling), numbers written as words, punctuation kept standard. Format: [Timestamp] [Speaker] [Content], tab-delimited.

02

QA

Review transcripts against the source audio for accuracy, formatting consistency, and correct handling of edge cases — stutters, cut-off words, letter-by-letter proper nouns, invalid-clip reasons.
